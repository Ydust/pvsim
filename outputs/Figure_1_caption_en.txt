Regional photovoltaic yield gains over crystalline silicon (c-Si). a,b, Single-junction gains at equal installed capacity, mapped and plotted against annual global horizontal irradiance (GHI). c,d, Corresponding tandem gains at equal aperture area. Each scatter includes 369 modelling regions; colour indicates mean air temperature on a shared scale. Dashed lines show descriptive linear fits; r and ρ are Pearson and Spearman correlations with GHI. GHI-quartile distributions are provided in Figures S104–S106.
