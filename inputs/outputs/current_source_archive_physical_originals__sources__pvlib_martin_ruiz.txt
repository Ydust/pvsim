251:     Returns
252:     -------
253:     iam : numeric
254:         The incident angle modifier(s)
255: 
256:     Notes
257:     -----
258:     `martin_ruiz` calculates the incidence angle modifier (IAM) as described in
259:     [1]_. The information required is the incident angle (AOI) and the angular
260:     losses coefficient (a_r). Note that [1]_ has a corrigendum [2]_ which
261:     clarifies a mix-up of 'alpha's and 'a's in the former.
262: 
263:     The incident angle modifier is defined as
264: 
265:     .. math::
266: 
267:        IAM = \frac{1 - \exp(-\frac{\cos(aoi)}{a_r})}
268:        {1 - \exp(\frac{-1}{a_r})}
269: 
270:     which is presented as :math:`AL(\alpha) = 1 - IAM` in equation 4 of [1]_,
271:     with :math:`\alpha` representing the angle of incidence AOI. Thus IAM = 1
272:     at AOI = 0°, and IAM = 0 at AOI = 90°.  This equation is only valid for
273:     0° <= aoi <= 90°, therefore `iam` is constrained to 0.0 outside this
274:     interval.
275: 
276:     References
277:     ----------
278:     .. [1] N. Martin and J. M. Ruiz, "Calculation of the PV modules angular
279:        losses under field conditions by means of an analytical model", Solar
280:        Energy Materials & Solar Cells, vol. 70, pp. 25-38, 2001.
281: 
282:     .. [2] N. Martin and J. M. Ruiz, "Corrigendum to 'Calculation of the PV
283:        modules angular losses under field conditions by means of an
284:        analytical model'", Solar Energy Materials & Solar Cells, vol. 110,
285:        pp. 154, 2013.
286: 
287:     See Also
288:     --------

290:     pvlib.iam.physical
291:     pvlib.iam.ashrae
