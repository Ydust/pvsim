

Annual-yield stress outputs
Annual_policy_S7_36 contains deterministic systematic annual-yield stresses and the domain of the policy counterexample. Annual_choice_certificates.csv has 16,176 city-level records; Annual_certificate_summary.csv aggregates them. Annual_yield_stress.csv has 864 portfolio-policy records (27 technology combinations per amplitude; central combinations repeat across four amplitudes). Counterexample_joint_scope.csv has 15,552 cases; Counterexample_yield_degradation_grid.csv has 1,944 cases. Annual_physical_bounds.csv contains exact bounds on capacity/aperture yield ratios under the declared independent box.

The four annual-yield amplitudes (1%, 2%, 5%, 10%) are stress levels, not measured error quantiles. delta_si/delta_sj/delta_t are fractional systematic changes repeated for the project lifetime. Three-level policy-grid extrema are sampled, not certified continuous-box bounds. choice_certified means the selected option has a strictly positive worst-case NPV margin over all competitors, including no build; an uncertified city need not change. For the scope grid, the lifetime and degradation factors are independently crossed, not a linked technology trajectory. O&M proportional means 1.5% of initial technology CAPEX per year; fixed values are USD/kW-year. Values 1.6 and 6.4 are half/double the existing 3.2 anchor, not extra observed estimates. All non-listed assumptions remain central; in particular c-Si/tandem CAPEX and durability remain fixed. The separate joint scope does not cross the annual perturbation grid.

discounted_mwh and undiscounted_mwh are changes relative to the reoptimized no-support portfolio in the same scenario. Entry and switching fields sum to the corresponding total; counterexample_physical additionally requires new_projects > 0. Negative energy without additional projects is not counted as this counterexample. Monetary fields are USD at the existing price base. Public spending includes the 5% administration assumption. The full box certificates permit city-specific errors; the 27-point policy tests use the same technology-specific bias across all cities. No frequencies in these grids are probabilities.

Run with code/run.py annual_policy. This needs no new downloads. It regenerates all seven CSVs and verification.json under outputs/annual_policy_robustness_20260910. The included baseline counterexample CSV is used only for regression verification, not as a computed output input. Before/after policies reoptimize choices; optional retirement also reoptimizes management. This systematic-bias exercise does not sample actual interannual weather or reconstruct empirical annual error from instantaneous response residuals.
